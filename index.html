FAQ

Questions we get asked before every project

Straight answers — no vague "it depends" without context. Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Ask us directly →
How do you scope a website project?

We evaluate the scope depending on pages, integrations (booking, WhatsApp, payment), and animation complexity. We always provide a clear, written proposal before starting — no open-ended retainers unless you ask for one.

How long does it take to build and launch?

A focused marketing website: 1–2 weeks. A multi-page site with integrations: 2–4 weeks. Custom software is scoped project-by-project — typically 4–8 weeks for a first working version. We provide a timeline in writing before work begins, and we respect it.

Who handles hosting and deployment?

We deploy to the platform that makes most sense — Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, or a VPS — and either set up accounts in your name or manage it for you directly. We never hold your hosting hostage to keep you as a client.

Do I own the code and design after the project?

Yes — fully and immediately on final payment. We hand over all source code, design files, domain access, and login credentials. You can take the project anywhere, host it yourself, or hire another developer. No proprietary locks, no dependencies that trap you.

What if something breaks after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free bug-fix support post-launch. If something we built breaks, we fix it — no charge, no argument. For ongoing support beyond that, we offer transparent maintenance packages with a clear scope of what's covered.

Can you work with a non-technical client?

That's most of our clients. We translate technical decisions into plain language at every stage. You'll never get a jargon-heavy status update — just clear progress reports, screenshots, and a preview link. Your job is to tell us what you need; our job is to handle the rest.
